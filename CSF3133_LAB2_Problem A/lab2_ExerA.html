About Our Event

FSKM Career Day 2026 is organised to help students explore career opportunities, learn from industry professionals, and prepare for their future careers.

Career Talk

Industry professionals will share their career experiences and provide useful advice for students entering the workforce.

Resume Writing Workshop

Students will learn how to prepare professional resumes and improve their job application skills.
